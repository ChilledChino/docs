# Group Plans & Scheduling

Chino Together helps small groups coordinate hangouts, game nights, study sessions, and watch parties across Discord and `/together`.

## Creating & Joining Plans

Use [`/together`](/docs/reference/commands) (`create`, `join`, `plans`) or `/together` on the web:

- **Plan Kinds:** `game`, `study`, or `watch` (or start from a saved template). Max title length: 80 characters.
- **Visibility & Invites:** Plans start `private`. Generate an invite code (with optional expiry and max uses) to make it `unlisted` and shareable.

## Availability Grid & Best Times

- **30-Minute Grid:** Mark when you are free on a 7-day, 30-minute granularity grid (up to 24 hours per slot) in your chosen timezone.
- **Best Times Overlap:** Once 2 or more members submit availability, Chino finds and ranks the best overlapping windows by most members, longest duration, and earliest start.
