# Bug Reports, Feedback & Tickets

Submit bug reports, feedback, or feature requests to the Chino team and track staff replies in Discord or on the web.

## Submitting a Ticket

- **In Discord:** Run [`/report`](/docs/reference/commands) `<type>` (`report`, `feedback`, or `feature`) anywhere (works in servers and DMs) and fill in the modal (Title `8–120` chars, Description `20–2,000` chars).
- **On the Web:** Use `/feedback` (supports signed-in users and guests via Turnstile).
- **Anti-Abuse:** Duplicate submissions are blocked, and per-user/guest rate limits apply.

## Viewing & Replying

- **In Discord ([`/tickets`](/docs/reference/commands)):** List your tickets or inspect a specific ticket key, reply via modal (`5–1,500` chars), and toggle **DM Follow-Up** notifications on/off.
- **In DMs:** When DM Follow-Up is enabled, staff replies arrive in DMs with a **Reply** button so you can respond without leaving Discord.
- **On the Web (`/tickets` & `/tickets/<id>`):** View status (`Open`, `Pending`, `Solved`, `Duplicate`, `Unavailable`), read the full thread, toggle DM follow-ups, and submit replies.
