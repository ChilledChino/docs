# Birthdays & Mini-Games

## Birthdays

Celebrate member birthdays automatically with [`/birthday`](/docs/reference/commands) (`mine`, `upcoming`, `settings`), [`/configure`](/docs/reference/commands) (`birthday`), or **Dashboard → Birthdays** (`/dashboard/<id>/birthdays`):

- **For Members:** Set your birthday (with optional birth year) and timezone in [`/birthday`](/docs/reference/commands) (`mine`), and browse upcoming server birthdays (`upcoming`).
- **For Admins:** Pick the announcement channel, optional birthday role ping, and custom template using `` `{users}` ``.

## Mini-Games & AFK

| Feature | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Quiz** ([`/quiz`](/docs/reference/commands)) | Trivia by category, 20-second timer, multiple-choice buttons, pays ChinoCoins and quest progress |
| **TypeRacer** ([`/typeracer`](/docs/reference/commands)) | Multiplayer typing speed race (`start`, `join`, `leave`, `discard`) tracking WPM |
| **AFK** ([`/afk`](/docs/reference/commands)) | Sets an AFK status with optional reason; notifies people who mention you (admins can clear AFKs in [`/mod`](/docs/reference/commands)) |

Admins can enable/disable Quiz and TypeRacer, lock them to specific channels, and set cooldowns in [`/configure`](/docs/reference/commands) (`minigames`) or **Dashboard → Mini-games** (`/dashboard/<id>/community`).
