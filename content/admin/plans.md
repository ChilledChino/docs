# Plans & Limits

Chino uses a **user-owned premium model**: your subscription belongs to your Discord account and can be shared to servers you choose via [`/account`](/docs/reference/commands) (`premium`) or `/user/subscription`.

For up to date data, see [Pricing and Limits](/docs/reference/pricing).

## Subscribing

Premium is a monthly PayPal subscription that renews by itself. Subscribe from `/premium`, the home page pricing, or `/user/subscription`.

| You want to                    | What happens                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Upgrade**                    | You only need to pay the price difference for the remainder of the month. The new tier starts immediately and the new price applies from the following renewal. |
| **Downgrade**                  | Nothing is charged and you get to keep your tier until the month ends. Afterwards, you switch.                                                                  |
| **Undo a downgrade**           | Revert back to your tier again. This is only possible before its month ends.                                                                                    |
| **Cancel**                     | **Cancel auto-renew** on `/user/subscription`. This stops PayPal from charging you again and you get to keep your premium until the month ends.                 |
| **Keep a month-by-month plan** | **Turn on auto-renew**. The first charge is when your current premium ends.                                                                                     |

- **Failed payment:** premium stays on for a few days while PayPal retries, and Chino sends you a DM.
- **Refund:** premium immediately ends when the payment is refunded.
- **Price changes:** your price stays the same until you change plans.

## Free Trials and Gifts

- **Free trial:** when trials are offered, start one from the plan window. No PayPal and no card. Only one trial period is possible per Discord account. To prevent abuse, brand new accounts are not allowed to start a free trial period.
- **Gift:** A free premium given to you for a set time.
- Subscribing during a free trial or gift costs nothing until it ends.

## Sharing Your Plan

- Share your plan to the servers you choose; how many servers you can share to depends on your tier.
- A server's limits (e.g. counters, voice rooms, role menus, shop items, and more) follow the plan shared to it.
- Your AI allowance follows your own plan; background AI features (e.g. music autoplay) use the server's allowance. See [AI Usage Limits](/docs/ai/limits).

Prices, tiers, and every limit are listed in [Pricing and Limits](/docs/reference/pricing).
