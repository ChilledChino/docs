# Plans & Limits

Chino uses a **user-owned premium model**: your subscription belongs to your Discord account and can be shared to servers you choose via [`/account`](/docs/reference/commands) (`premium`) or `/user/subscription`.

For up to date data, see [Pricing and Limits](/docs/reference/pricing).
