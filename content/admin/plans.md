# Plans & Limits

Chino uses a **user-owned premium model**: your subscription belongs to your Discord account and can be shared to servers you choose via [`/account`](/docs/reference/commands) (`premium`) or `/user/subscription`.

For up to date data, see [Pricing and Limits](/docs/reference/pricing).

## Subscribing

Premium is a monthly PayPal subscription that renews by itself. Subscribe from `/premium`, the home page pricing, or `/user/subscription`.

| You want to | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Upgrade** | Pay only the difference for the rest of this month. The new tier starts now; the new price from your next renewal. |
| **Downgrade** | Nothing charged now. You keep your tier until the paid month ends, then switch. |
| **Undo a downgrade** | Pick your current tier again before the month ends. |
| **Cancel** | **Cancel auto-renew** on `/user/subscription`. PayPal stops charging; you keep premium until the paid month ends. |
| **Keep a month-by-month plan** | **Turn on auto-renew**. The first charge is when your current premium ends. |

- **Failed payment:** premium stays on for a few days while PayPal retries, and Chino sends you a DM.
- **Refund:** premium ends when the payment is refunded.
- **Price changes:** your price stays the same until you change plan.

## Free Trials and Gifts

- **Free trial:** when trials are offered, start one from the plan window. No PayPal and no card. One per Discord account, ever, and not for brand new accounts.
- **Gift:** premium given to you for a set time, with no payment.
- Subscribing during a trial or gift costs nothing until it ends.

## Sharing Your Plan

- Share your plan to the servers you choose; how many servers you can share to depends on your tier.
- A server's limits (counters, voice rooms, role menus, shop items and more) follow the plan shared to it.
- Your AI allowance follows your own plan; background AI features (like music autoplay) use the server's allowance. See [AI Usage Limits](/docs/ai/limits).

Prices, tiers and every limit are listed in [Pricing and Limits](/docs/reference/pricing).
