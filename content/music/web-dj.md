# Web DJ & Personal Playlists

The standalone Web DJ at `/music` lets you control server playback, browse charts, watch synced lyrics, and manage personal playlists from any browser.

![Standalone Web DJ at /music](/images/feature_music_web_standalone.webm)

## Web DJ (`/music`)

- **Views:** Home (charts, playlists, recently played), Search, Now Playing, Playlists, Charts, Artist/Album pages.
- **Right Panel:** Switch between **Up Next** (with drag/reorder and history), **Lyrics Stage** (smooth synced lyrics), and **Activity** (recent player actions).
- **Live Sync:** Updates automatically across all open tabs and devices.
- **Voice Co-Presence Gate:** Controls stay locked until you join the same Discord voice channel as Chino. Leaving the voice channel immediately disables controls.

## Personal Playlists

Save and reuse your favorite queues across any server:

- **In Discord:** Open [`/music`](/docs/reference/commands) → **Playlists** to save the current queue, play/append a saved playlist, or delete playlists.
- **On the Web:** Manage playlists at `/music/playlists` and launch playback into your current server.
